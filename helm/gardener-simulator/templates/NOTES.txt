Gardener Simulator has been deployed!

1. Get the kubeconfig to access the simulator:

   curl http://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.managementNodePort }}/management/kubeconfig > kubeconfig.yaml

2. Use kubectl with the kubeconfig:

   kubectl --kubeconfig=kubeconfig.yaml get shoots -n garden-project

3. Access the Management API:

   # Get status
   curl http://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.managementNodePort }}/management/status

   # Add a shoot
   curl -X POST http://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.managementNodePort }}/management/shoots \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{"namespace":"garden-project","name":"new-cluster","cloudType":"aws"}'

4. Service endpoints:
   - Gardener API: https://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.apiNodePort }}
   - Management API: http://<node-ip>:{{ .Values.service.managementNodePort }}/management/
